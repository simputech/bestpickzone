# Retinol vs bakuchiol — research and draft scope

Status: research checked; draft requires independent review before publication.

## Evidence

- https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29947134/ — primary randomized double-blind trial, published BJD 2019 (online 2018): 44 participants, 12 weeks, 0.5% bakuchiol twice daily versus 0.5% retinol daily. Both reduced assessed wrinkles/hyperpigmentation without significant between-group difference; retinol group reported more scaling/stinging. This does not demonstrate equivalence for all formulas or prescription retinoids, establish pregnancy safety, or supply a universal onset timeline.
- https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/anti-aging/retinoid-retinol — AAD clinical guidance distinguishes OTC retinol from other retinoids; cautions about irritation, inflammation and pregnancy; suggests gentle introduction, moisturizer and sun protection. Do not transfer tretinoin concentrations or evidence to OTC retinol.
- https://health.clevelandclinic.org/bakuchiol — dermatologist guidance: bakuchiol may be gentler but irritation/allergy remain possible; pregnancy safety requires more research. Botanical origin is not proof of pregnancy safety.
- https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-secrets/routine/pregnancy-skin-care — advises avoiding retinoids in pregnancy and discussing treatment plans during pregnancy/breastfeeding with clinicians.
