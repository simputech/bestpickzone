# Microcurrent device identity and purchase constraints

Checked October 18, 2026. Editorial research; no hands-on or comparative clinical effectiveness claim. The article distinguishes current TRINITY+ Starter Kit, standard BEAR 2 and current MyoLift QT Plus. It does not repeat unsupported lifting percentages, medical outcomes or safety assurances.

- NuFACE [Starter Kit](https://www.mynuface.com/products/trinity-starter-kit-microcurrent-facial-toning-device): identify the exact Starter package, not Classic or Complete. [Manufacturer-linked manual](https://www.mynuface.com/cdn/shop/files/NF_TrinityMini_UserManual_Global-4.pdf?v=1785844890582974176) supplies treatment-area restrictions and contraindications, including pregnancy, seizure history, implanted electronics and other conditions. Do not infer eligibility from a buying comparison.
- FOREO [BEAR 2 collection](https://www.foreo.com/bear-2-collection) and [BEAR 2 manual](https://www.foreo.com/manuals/bear-2): standard facial device, not go/body/eyes & lips. Initial app registration/unlock and conductive product required. Manual restrictions supersede broad marketing wording such as face-and-neck. No comparative proof of superiority inferred from proprietary Anti-Shock marketing.
- 7E [MyoLift QT Plus](https://www.7ewellness.com/products/at-home-microcurrent-devices-myolift-qt-plus-facial-toning) and [support index](https://help.7ewellness.com/en-US/articles/myolift-qt-plus-15733): current QT Plus, not old QT or professional Mini. Base and Premium differ in app access. Base trial does not mean permanent full access; limited MyPlan/App Free operation is distinct from a subscription. Full current manual not independently located; article explicitly requires obtaining it before deciding suitability. No unverified exact Amazon package offered; official store first.

Newly mapped NuFACE and FOREO API candidates remain subject to strict returned identity checks and live QA. MyoLift remains a documented partial-route exception.
