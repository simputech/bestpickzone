## Pressure cookers

- CPSC recall 25-247: https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/2025/SharkNinja-Recalls-1-8-Million-Foodi-Multi-Function-Pressure-Cookers-Due-to-Burn-Hazard-Serious-Burn-Injuries-Reported . Opened. May 1, 2025 OP300 family recall; pressure lid may open while pressurized. Stop pressure function, obtain free replacement lid. Original article promoted generic Foodi 9-in-1/6.5-quart without addressing this.
- Manufacturer remedy: https://www.rqa-inc.com/client/sharkninja/ . Opened and used its link to resolve CPSC. Includes OP300 replacement lid SKU111FY300; discard affected pressure lid per official instructions; other functions remain allowed by recall guidance. No recalled Foodi purchase CTA or API pick in new draft.
- Standard Duo6QT primary: https://instantpot.com/products/instant-pot-duo-6qt-multi-use-pressure-cooker . Opened. SKU112-0170-01, seven functions, six-quart stainless pot, US120V. No air frying. Exact model required in commerce; other Duo variants are not substitutes. Avoided safest/fastest and invented community or cooking-time testimonial.
