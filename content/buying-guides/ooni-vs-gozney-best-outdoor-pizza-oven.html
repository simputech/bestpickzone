Compare equivalent models before comparing brands

The Ooni Koda 2 is a 14-inch gas oven. It is different from the older Koda 12 and Koda 16, and from the larger Koda 2 Pro and Max. The Gozney Arc is also a 14-inch gas oven; Arc XL is the larger 16-inch model. A search result or discount with a similar name is not necessarily the same product.

These two models provide a useful starting comparison for a buyer making one moderate-size pizza at a time. They do not represent every Ooni or Gozney design. Someone seeking wood firing, a larger cooking area or indoor operation needs a different shortlist. Do not treat a premium label, a wider brand range or a photograph of a party as evidence of better results for your own cooking.

Confirm the location before choosing either oven

These gas ovens are outdoor cooking appliances. Do not operate them inside a home, garage or other enclosed space. A balcony, covered patio or apartment terrace is not automatically suitable simply because it is outdoors. Check the exact manual, building rules and applicable local restrictions before buying; do not infer permission from this comparison.

The Arc manual prohibits operation beneath a combustible structure and specifies clearance around the oven. Follow its complete regional instructions rather than transferring a distance from another model or country. The Koda 2 needs its own placement check. A counter that holds the oven's feet is not enough if the surroundings, overhead area or route past the oven are unsuitable.

Plan a stable approved surface, access to the gas controls, room to handle a peel and a place to set cooked food. Keep children and pets away from the cooking area. Do not move an oven while hot or improvise a fuel conversion. If you cannot establish a permitted setup, postpone the purchase instead of trying to make a gas model fit an unsuitable location.

Think through serving, not just baking

A one-pizza oven requires a workflow: prepare dough, organize toppings, launch, turn as instructed, remove and serve. If one person cooks while everyone else eats, decide whether that is part of the fun or a reason to use your existing kitchen oven. A maximum temperature or short advertised bake time does not include dough preparation, preheating, recovery and cleanup.

For a small household, individual pizzas served in succession may suit the meal. For a larger group wanting to eat simultaneously, plan how to stage food without assuming the oven's size label proves adequate throughput. We have not measured either oven's output across repeated launches, and no party-capacity claim is implied here.

Storage matters too. Decide where the oven will cool and where it will be kept when not used, following the manufacturer's instructions. A cover is not evidence that every location or weather condition is acceptable. If moving the unit is part of every cooking session, consider the full handling route and get the exact weight and lifting guidance before ordering.

When to choose a different category

If you want wood firing, compare models expressly designed and configured for it. Neither brand name makes a gas-only oven suitable for adding solid fuel. If you need indoor cooking, research a specifically approved electric indoor model and its clearances. An electric oven still needs approval for the intended location and its own installation check.

If larger pizza is the main goal, compare larger models directly rather than stretching dough beyond the named oven's intended size. If you are unsure how often you will make pizza, use existing equipment first and learn which limitation actually bothers you. Buying a specialized oven is easier to justify when you can name the task it improves.
