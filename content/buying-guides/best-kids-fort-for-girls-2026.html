Make a room plan before adding decorations

Once the exact assembled dimensions are confirmed, mark the intended footprint on the floor using a room-safe method. Include the entrance approach and the space needed for an adult to supervise and assist. Check that the tent does not block a doorway, route through the room, heater or access to other furniture. Plan around normal use, not just the spot where it fits for a photo.

Keep the arrangement simple at first. Cushions and rugs occupy space and can change how easily children enter and move around. Use only age-appropriate items and follow the tent's instructions. Do not add heavy blankets, hanging objects or other loads to a frame unless the manufacturer explicitly permits them. A decorative play tent is not climbing equipment.

The included star lights are an accessory with their own setup questions. Check the supplied instructions, power arrangement and cord routing, and keep cords and accessible components out of children's reach as directed. Do not assume inclusion proves that any improvised installation is appropriate. Leaving the lights unused is an option if a suitable arrangement cannot be established.
