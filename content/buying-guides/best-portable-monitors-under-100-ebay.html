A portable monitor under $100 can be a useful second screen, but the budget only works when the display connects to your computer and arrives with the equipment you need. Treat $100 as a shopping ceiling, not a verified current offer. The eBay searches below can contain items above that amount, damaged units, accessories and models with very different connection requirements.

Begin with the laptop, not the discount

Write down the exact computer model and available video outputs. A USB-C connector does not by itself promise video. Check the computer's documentation for the relevant port's capabilities, then match those to the monitor manual. Also check which cable is supplied: a charging-only cable can look like a video-capable USB-C cable while being unsuitable for the job.

The original Lenovo ThinkVision M14 is a useful specific example. Lenovo's specification lists a 14-inch 1920×1080 screen and two USB-C ports supporting DisplayPort 1.2 Alternate Mode and USB Power Delivery. It is not a generic HDMI screen. Do not assume that an M14t, M14d or another ThinkVision listing has identical features because part of its name matches.

The ASUS ZenScreen MB16ACV illustrates another path: ASUS documents hybrid USB-C/USB-A connectivity, with the USB-A path requiring a DisplayLink driver. That can matter on a work computer where you cannot install software. These facts apply to the MB16ACV, not every ZenScreen. A listing that omits its suffix leaves a meaningful compatibility question unanswered.

Compare five search routes without assuming a winner

Generic 15.6-inch displays

A generic new display may be worth comparing when its exact ports, manual and return conditions are clear. There is no verified model behind the category name, so claims about brightness, color coverage, stand quality or one-cable use need evidence for the actual item. Do not infer 1080p resolution from the diagonal screen size alone.

Search eBay for new 15.6-inch USB-C portable monitors. Filter to your delivered budget and read the description for included cables and power requirements. A low asking price is not proof of complete equipment.

Lenovo ThinkVision M14 candidates

Use the exact original M14 specification as a comparison reference only when the seller confirms that model. Request photographs of the label, panel and ports. A refurbished label does not establish that the display has been tested for pressure marks, dead pixels or intermittent connections; ask what the condition assessment covered.

Search eBay for refurbished ThinkVision M14 monitors. If your computer lacks the required video-capable USB-C output, stop before treating this as the obvious branded upgrade.

ASUS ZenScreen candidates

ZenScreen is a family name, not a single connection standard. Identify the model suffix before comparing it with the MB16ACV example. Some results can include different inputs, stands or touch features. Confirm the compatible operating systems and any software installation requirements in the manual, particularly for a managed employer device.

Search eBay for refurbished ASUS ZenScreen monitors. We have not confirmed that any result currently meets the $100 ceiling or includes the expected cable and support.

Open-box budget brands

The Arzopa, InnoView and KYY search is a category exploration, not a verified recommendation of three interchangeable models. Open-box condition can mean an unused return, missing accessories or visible wear. Compare the actual unit and package contents. Ask whether the illustrated folio or kickstand is included and whether it supports the orientation you intend to use.

Search eBay for open-box Arzopa, InnoView and KYY monitors. Replace a vague brand-level comparison with the complete model number before deciding.

ViewSonic and AOC candidates

These results can broaden your comparison, but an unfamiliar bargain is not automatically better value than the first listing. Verify the input and power arrangement, not simply the name on the bezel. An older USB display may require software or a connection path that differs from a newer USB-C video monitor.

Search eBay for refurbished ViewSonic and AOC portable monitors. Exact specifications and compatibility remain unverified until matched to an identifiable model and its primary documentation.

For example, imagine a laptop with HDMI output and USB-C used only for charging. An original M14 is not made compatible merely by buying a cheap cable with the two connector shapes. Conversion direction and power matter. Compare a display with a documented HDMI input and suitable power arrangement, or obtain exact compatibility confirmation before buying an active conversion device.

Conversely, a laptop with a compatible video-capable USB-C port may support a straightforward USB-C setup, but the monitor's power needs still have to be met. Test your intended arrangement during the return period. Do not assume that a monitor can charge the laptop just because both have USB-C ports.

Make the $100 ceiling include everything

Calculate the item price plus shipping, applicable tax and essential missing accessories. A hypothetical $75 screen with $15 shipping and a $15 required cable is already $105 before tax; that is an arithmetic example, not a current offer. A listing priced at $99 may similarly exceed your ceiling after checkout costs.

Compare the complete setup with a conventional used desk monitor if portability is occasional. A portable display can save packed space, but a separate stand, charger and cables change the benefit. Measure the packed dimensions against your bag and the usable stand depth against your desk. The larger diagonal is not automatically the more usable travel choice.

Inspect the panel and return terms

Ask for photographs of the actual screen powered on, including a light background where marks may be visible. This can reveal obvious damage but cannot certify the absence of every pixel fault. Clarify the seller's stated condition, what counts as a returnable defect and who pays return shipping. Avoid treating “tested” as a comprehensive test report.

On arrival, inspect before discarding packaging. Use the intended computer and connection path to check stable operation, stand support, controls and readable text at your preferred scaling. Try the tasks you actually bought it for, such as a spreadsheet beside a document. You do not need a claim about professional color accuracy to decide whether ordinary text work fits your needs.

Decision and primary sources

Choose the compatible, complete and adequately documented unit within your total budget.
