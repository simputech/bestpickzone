For Taylor Jenkins Reid's novels in publication order, begin with Forever, Interrupted (2013) and continue through Atmosphere (2025). You can also choose a first book by premise: The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo for Hollywood, Daisy Jones & The Six for a fictional band, or Carrie Soto Is Back for a tennis comeback. These are reading options, not a requirement to finish every earlier novel. The Last Days of Vic & Coco is announced for March 2, 2027 and is listed separately below.

Choose a starting point without buying the entire catalog

Publication order is useful if you want to compare Reid's changing subjects and narrative structures. If you mainly want one book, choose the premise that interests you. Hollywood, music, family relationships and competitive sport offer different ways into the catalog; a familiar bestseller is not automatically the right match for every reader.

Our suggested first comparison is Evelyn Hugo versus Daisy Jones. Both concern public figures and private stories, but one centers a film star's account and the other uses an ensemble interview structure. Read a sample of each if narrative format matters more to you than the setting.

1. Forever, Interrupted: the debut and its grief-centered premise

Atria's record dates the debut July 9, 2013. Elsie and Ben marry after a rapid courtship; his death brings Elsie together with a mother-in-law who did not know about the marriage. The story interweaves the relationship with the aftermath of loss.

Choose it to begin at the debut or because grief and an unexpected family relationship interest you. The premise is not a light newlywed romance. A reader seeking glamorous celebrity settings may prefer a later title, but that preference does not make the earlier novel merely homework.

2. After I Do: a relationship under reassessment

The publisher's 2014 record and author's synopsis describe Lauren and Ryan taking a year apart when their marriage reaches a breaking point. It is a different relationship question from the sudden bereavement in the debut.

Consider it when you want fiction about what follows the initial romance. The characters' arrangement is a story premise, not relationship advice for readers. For a group, compare what each person expects from the separation with what the decision reveals about their assumptions.

Search Amazon for After I Do editions.

3. Maybe in Another Life: compare two possible paths

The publisher identifies the 2015 novel and its parallel outcomes after Hannah faces a decision at the end of an evening. Choose it if the appeal is examining how a life might unfold differently rather than following a single linear relationship story.

The branching structure is the key tradeoff. If you enjoy comparing alternatives, it gives you a concrete reason to select this title. If switching between possibilities interrupts your immersion, sample the transitions before purchasing. We do not assume the structure makes it faster or easier for every reader.

Search Amazon for Maybe in Another Life editions.

4. One True Loves: an unresolved past returns

Atria's page dates the novel June 7, 2016. A woman faces a choice between the husband she believed dead and the fiancé who became part of her changed life. That is the premise, rather than evidence that one kind of love is universally more valid.

Choose it for an emotionally difficult relationship dilemma. It is less appropriate if your immediate reading preference is to avoid disappearance, grief or romantic conflict. Check that a search result is the novel itself rather than adaptation-related material or an unofficial summary.

Search Amazon for One True Loves editions.

5. The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo: public image and a private account

The publisher's original hardcover record gives June 13, 2017. A reclusive Hollywood star chooses journalist Monique Grant to hear her life story. The contrast between a public reputation and the account behind it is the reason to start here.

This is our suggested entry for readers drawn to fame, ambition and the telling of a life. It is fiction, not a biography of an actual film star. Avoid detailed plot summaries if you want to encounter the reasons for Evelyn's choices through the novel itself.

Search Amazon for The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo editions.

6. Daisy Jones & The Six: a different narrative format

The publisher's record dates the novel March 5, 2019 and describes a fictional 1970s rock band's rise and breakup through an interview-style narrative. Choose it when multiple recollections and voices appeal to you.

The format is worth sampling in both print and audio if you are deciding between them. A cast of voices can be appealing, but it does not establish that the recording is everyone's best format. Nor should a convincing fictional oral history be mistaken for an actual band's documented story.

Search Amazon for Daisy Jones & The Six editions.

7. Malibu Rising: a family ensemble

The original US hardcover appeared June 1, 2021. The story centers four Riva siblings and a party in August 1983. Choose it when family relationships within a celebrity-adjacent setting interest you more than an interview-based band history.

There are recurring names and connections in Reid's celebrity novels, but a reader need not buy a complete bundle to try this premise. Publication order is a convenient way to encounter connections in sequence if that is part of your enjoyment.

Search Amazon for Malibu Rising editions.

8. Carrie Soto Is Back: competition and legacy

The publisher's format records place the original release on August 30, 2022. Carrie returns to tennis to defend the record and reputation she built. This is the starting point to consider if athletic ambition is more compelling to you than Hollywood or music.

Read it as a fictional athlete's story, not a training guide or a verified portrait of every professional player's experience. The focus on competitive identity offers a different discussion question: what remains of a person's sense of self when a record can be surpassed?

Search Amazon for Carrie Soto Is Back editions.

9. Atmosphere: the next published novel

The publisher dates the US hardcover June 3, 2025. Reid's synopsis follows Joan Goodwin through the space-shuttle program of the 1980s, connecting professional ambition with a love story.

It belongs after Carrie Soto in publication order. Choose it for this setting and relationship premise, without treating the fictional events as a NASA mission record. A later paperback release does not make it a new 2026 novel.
