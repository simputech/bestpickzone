The Real Difference

Night Repair vs. All-Day Hydration.

This comparison comes down to when and how you want to treat your lips. The Laneige Lip Sleeping Mask is an overnight occlusive treatment — it seals moisture in with berry-derived antioxidants and vitamin C while you sleep. It's the better choice for genuinely dry, cracked, or peeling lips that need active repair.

The Rhode Peptide Lip Treatment is a daytime staple — a sheer, glossy balm containing peptides, shea butter, and a vitamin E complex, designed to be reapplied throughout the day. It delivers hydration and plumping ingredients in a format that actually looks good on, fits in a pocket, and has generated one of the most devoted skincare communities of the last two years.
