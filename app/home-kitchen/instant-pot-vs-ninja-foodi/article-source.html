The Actual Difference

This Comes Down to One Question: Do You Need to Air Fry?

The Instant Pot Duo 7-in-1 has over 200,000 Amazon reviews — more than almost any kitchen product ever listed. That number isn't hype; it's the accumulated verdict of a decade of real home cooks. It pressure cooks, slow cooks, sautés, steams, makes rice, yogurt, and warms food. At $79.99, it's one of the most cost-effective appliances in a modern kitchen.

The Ninja Foodi 9-in-1 does everything the Instant Pot does — and adds a crisping lid that air fries. You can pressure cook ribs until tender, then swap to the air crisping lid and finish them with a caramelized crust in minutes, all in the same pot. That's genuinely impressive. It also costs $70 more.

The decision, bluntly, is this: if you already own an air fryer (or don't want one), the Instant Pot wins by a mile. If you want to replace an air fryer — or combine both into one appliance — the Ninja Foodi is worth the premium.
