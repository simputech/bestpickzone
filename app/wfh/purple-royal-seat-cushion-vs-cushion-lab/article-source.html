Why the Purple Grid Doesn't Flatten — and Why Foam Does

Memory and high-density foam cushions work by deforming under weight to create a contoured surface. The problem is that repeated compression cycles break down the foam cell structure over months, reducing the cushion's height and support. The Purple Grid is a hyper-elastic polymer that returns to its original shape after each compression — the same mechanism that makes it effective on day one keeps it effective on day 500. This is the core reason for the $40 price difference.
