What to Buy With a Bambino Plus—and What to Keep

Updated October 18, 2026

By BestPickZone Editors · Research checked October 18, 2026

With a Breville Bambino Plus, buy a suitable espresso grinder if you need one, then a scale that fits your cup and tray. Use the supplied tamper, baskets and milk jug before shopping for replacements. A bottomless portafilter, specialty basket and oversized accessory kit are optional decisions, not requirements for starting. The useful purchase order depends on the coffee you use and the problem you encounter.

This guide covers the US BES500 Bambino Plus, not the base Bambino BES450. Its automatic milk workflow makes some seemingly ordinary accessories consequential: Breville's current manual specifies the supplied stainless-steel jug. A generic “54mm kit” also does not prove that every included part fits the machine's portafilter interface or your chosen basket.

What is already in the Bambino Plus box?

Check the package before duplicating it. Breville's US listing includes a tamper, Razor dosing tool, milk jug, filter baskets and cleaning accessories. Regional or older packages can differ, so reconcile the actual contents with the instructions supplied with your unit. A missing part is a seller/support question before it becomes an upgrade opportunity.

Lay those parts out and identify their jobs. Keep the cleaning tools somewhere you can find them after the novelty of unboxing passes. Set aside the baskets you are not currently using rather than switching them accidentally between drinks. The goal is a predictable starting setup, not a counter covered with every item that can be advertised beside the machine.

Do not order a second pitcher simply because an accessory list describes one as essential. The current US/Canada manual says to use the supplied stainless-steel milk jug and explains contact with the temperature sensor during automatic texturing. If it is missing or damaged, ask Breville for the correct replacement rather than assuming a decorative pitcher is equivalent.

Does your coffee plan require a new grinder?

If you intend to grind fresh beans for the single-wall basket and lack a suitable grinder, that purchase deserves attention before cosmetic accessories. If you use pre-ground coffee with the appropriate dual-wall basket, you can start with that workflow instead. Breville's manual distinguishes those basket uses; they are not simply two quality grades of the same part.

The original Baratza Encore ESP is a concrete electric-grinder shortlist. Baratza describes espresso-focused adjustment and a supplied dosing cup. Check the ESP name carefully; standard Encore and ESP Pro specifications should not be mixed into this purchase.

Plan where you will collect and transfer the grounds. A grinder's ability to grind coffee does not mean every portafilter can hang directly beneath it. Cup-based transfer is an option, but try the movement with your work area in mind. Leave room to place the portafilter securely, distribute the coffee and tamp without knocking the cup or pulling on a cable.

Keep a grinder that already supports your recipe. This is not a claim that the Encore ESP outperforms a more expensive or different grinder you own. If you want more options, our Bambino grinder guide separates cup-based and timed hopper workflows.

  • Pros: an identifiable espresso model; a straightforward external addition without altering the machine.
  • Cons: dialing in and transfer still take practice; adds counter space; unnecessary if your current grinder works.

Skip this if you are content with your present coffee workflow or already have a suitable grinder.

Which scale fits the drink you actually make?

Choose by the combined scale-and-cup clearance under your installed portafilter, not by the word “espresso” in a listing. The Timemore Black Mirror Nano, white is a named option, with weight and timing functions documented on its manufacturer page. It is not a guaranteed fit for every tall cup.

Measure the tray's usable support area and the distance below the spouts. Add the height of the cup you will place on the scale, and allow space to insert and remove it. Check that the display stays visible and the side controls remain reachable. A small espresso cup and a tall mug create different constraints even on the same machine.

A scale can help you record drink weight while the machine's programmed shot controls remain convenient. Do not interpret a button label as an exact beverage mass for every coffee and grind setting. Start by observing your current recipe rather than immediately replacing the machine's normal controls with an elaborate automatic-scale routine.

Skip this if an existing readable scale already fits. If the Nano does not fit, compare the actual dimensions of alternatives in our low-profile scale guide; the Nano, Mini and Basic are separate products.

When should you consider a tamper, basket or bottomless handle?

Only after identifying a specific limitation. A different tamper may offer a grip or leveling arrangement you prefer, but the base must suit the basket. “54mm” is the machine's nominal portafilter family; it does not mean a tamper with that exact measured diameter is necessarily the best fit for every replacement basket.

For a bottomless handle, request explicit BES500 compatibility and check the locking lugs, gasket interface, basket retention and depth. Do not buy a generic handle based solely on diameter. Its open underside changes visibility and clearance, but it does not automatically improve extraction. If your present shots are acceptable, there is no obligation to replace the supplied spouted handle.

A replacement basket changes another part of the recipe. Confirm its intended dose and whether it sits correctly in the handle before buying. Avoid changing basket, grind, dose and tamper together: you would have several explanations for any difference. We have not verified a universal aftermarket 54mm accessory set, so none is offered as a substitute for those checks.

Which small accessories can wait?

A knock box earns space if it makes disposing of used pucks easier in your kitchen. Measure its working area and the route to the sink or bin. A tamping mat is useful if it provides a stable protected preparation spot, but it should not become a reason to obstruct the drip tray or sensor area. A dosing funnel must fit the basket and be removed when the instructions require it.

Keep cleaning and descaling supplies tied to Breville's instructions. They serve different tasks; do not choose a chemical because it is marketed for espresso generally. Store supplies with their labels and keep the machine's cleaning tools accessible. A maintenance purchase can be more useful than another styling accessory, but it still needs the right product and procedure.

Start here: inventory the box, choose the basket for your coffee, and solve the grinder or weighing gap that actually exists. Skip this first: a second milk jug, generic portafilter bundle or several precision accessories bought before your first repeatable recipe.
