## Scope and operating rule

- **Inventory:** 100 dynamic book-review articles from `lib/books-data-part1.ts` through `lib/books-data-part5.ts`.
- **Shared issue:** The shared route at `app/books/[slug]/page.tsx` repeats book summaries in a "Visual map" and again in "Full reviews". The fix must remove this duplicate presentation for every dynamic book URL without reducing the purchase path.
- **Workflow standard:** Each route receives a structured relevance, duplication, and Amazon-conversion review before release. A page passes only when its content is topic-specific, has no drafting/SEO-instruction leakage, keeps clear FTC disclosure, and preserves a tracked Amazon purchase action.
- **Tracker protocol:** Update this file and the companion workbook after every completed batch. Do not mark a batch complete until the audit report and scoped validation are recorded.
